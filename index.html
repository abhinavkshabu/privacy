Stick Stick — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Stick Stick does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Your stickers, notes, and settings never leave your browser.

1. What Data We Store

Stick Stick stores the following data locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage API (chrome.storage.local):

  • Your sticker library — images, GIFs, and videos you upload or paste (stored as data URLs)
  • Sticker placements — the position, size, rotation, and layer order of stickers you place on each webpage
  • Sticky note content — text and color of notes you create
  • Extension settings — Edit Mode toggle state

2. What Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, location)
  • No browsing history or page content
  • No analytics, tracking, or telemetry
  • No cookies (beyond Chrome's internal storage)
  • No crash reports or usage statistics
  • No user accounts or authentication

3. Third-Party Services

Stick Stick connects to one external service: GIPHY.

When you search for GIFs or stickers in the GIF tab, your search query is sent to the GIPHY API to retrieve results. This is governed by GIPHY's Privacy Policy.

No other external services are contacted. Sticker images from GIPHY are downloaded and stored locally as data URLs, so they work offline after the initial download.

4. Permissions Explained

  • activeTab — Allows placing stickers on the page you're currently viewing
  • clipboardRead — Allows pasting images directly from your clipboard
  • storage / unlimitedStorage — Stores your sticker library and placements locally in your browser
  • Host permissions (giphy.com) — Required to fetch GIF search results and download GIF images

5. Data Retention

All data is stored in your browser's local storage and persists until you:

  • Manually clear stickers from a page (using the clear button)
  • Remove stickers from your library
  • Uninstall the extension
  • Clear your browser data

6. Data Sharing

Stick Stick does not share, sell, or transmit any of your data to any party. Everything stays on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Stick Stick does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else. No personal data is collected at all.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised accordingly.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the GitHub repository or reach out via the Chrome Web Store support page.
